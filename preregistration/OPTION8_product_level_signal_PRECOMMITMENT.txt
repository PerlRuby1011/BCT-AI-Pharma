OPTION8 — PER-PRODUCT ROUTING SIGNAL: DESIGN PRE-COMMITMENT
Written: 2026-08-25, BEFORE any code was written or any number observed.
Status: DESIGN FROZEN AT SECTION 3 BEFORE FIRST EXECUTION.

This document exists to satisfy the Section 4 constraint of
FIX_PROMPT_product_level_signal.md: the signal's existence and magnitude
are chosen for real-world plausibility and written down BEFORE any
false-positive rate is observed. If the magnitude is later changed, that
change and its reason must be appended here, and the original result
reported alongside.

======================================================================
1. WHY A DATA-GENERATION CHANGE IS NEEDED
======================================================================

Two prior fixes failed for the same structural reason:

  OPTION5 (empirical-Bayes shrinkage)  — clean null; all 12 organizations
      sit at a clip boundary, so shrinking a clipped value changes nothing.
  OPTION7 (chain-length-normalized aggregation) — improves ranking
      substantially (AUC 0.915 -> 0.971 design seeds, 0.913 -> 0.970
      held-out) but does NOT improve FPR at the registered operating point
      (worse by 1.53 pp design / 1.97 pp held-out, 5/5 held-out seeds).

Root cause: trust is a property of an ORGANIZATION; the label is a
property of a PRODUCT. The hot organization's burn-in counterfeit rate is
~12%, so 88% of its traffic is legitimate and inherits an identical
penalty. Measured directly: mean S1 is 0.6384 for clean products that
touch the hot org against 0.6037 for counterfeits — nearly
indistinguishable. Restricted to that ambiguous population, the best
available provenance feature has AUC 0.64.

No rescaling or reaggregation of per-organization trust can separate two
products that shared a custody hop, because the current generator gives
counterfeit rows no per-product observable at all beyond
``anomaly_severity`` (which feeds only the CNN's synthetic image).

======================================================================
2. THE SIGNAL, AND WHY THIS ONE
======================================================================

CHOSEN: shortcut routing — counterfeit products traverse FEWER
intermediate custody handoffs than legitimate products.

Real-world justification, fixed in advance:

Counterfeit pharmaceuticals predominantly enter the legitimate supply
chain at the wholesale distribution tier rather than at manufacture.
Mackey and Nayyar (2017), already cited in the manuscript, attribute 67%
of incidents to the wholesale distributor level, against 22% to
international transit and 11% to manufacturing. A product injected at the
wholesale tier has, by construction, not traversed the manufacturer ->
distributor leg that a legitimate product of the same type traverses. The
observable consequence is a custody chain that is shorter by approximately
one upstream handoff.

This is the mechanism the manuscript already invokes to explain why
provenance matters at all, so using it here introduces no new assumption
about counterfeit behaviour that the paper does not already assert.

MAGNITUDE, FIXED NOW: exactly ONE skipped handoff per counterfeit product,
applied by removing one upstream (manufacturer- or distributor-origin)
custody row for products carrying a counterfeit-labelled transaction.

Why one and not two, or a distribution: the 67%-at-wholesale figure
identifies a single tier of entry, which corresponds to a single skipped
upstream leg. Two would assert entry below the distributor tier, which the
same source puts at a much smaller share. A fitted distribution would have
free parameters, and free parameters are exactly what must not be tuned
here. One handoff is the value the cited mechanism implies, and it is not
adjustable without changing the justification.

PRE-COMMITMENT: this magnitude will NOT be changed in response to any
observed TPR, FPR, or AUC. If the result is a null, the null is reported.

======================================================================
3. DETECTOR FEATURE (frozen with the perturbation)
======================================================================

New per-product observable, read from the transaction table only:

    hop_deficit(p) = median_chain_length(drug_class(p)) - chain_length(p)

computed on the BURN-IN half for the class median, applied to the
evaluation half, so no evaluation-half label or outcome informs it. The
feature reads ``product_id``, ``drug_class`` and custody-row counts. It
never reads ``is_anomaly`` or ``anomaly_type``.

It enters the score as a bounded per-product multiplier on S1:

    S1' = S1 * (1 - beta * clip(hop_deficit / median_chain_length, 0, 1))

with beta = 0.30, fixed now. Rationale for beta: it is the largest value
that leaves a maximally-shortcut product (hop_deficit == median) with a
non-zero provenance score, preserving the property that provenance
degrades a score rather than zeroing it — the same design property the
existing penalty (0.10, capped at 3.0 excess -> trust floor 0.70)
already has. It is not selected against any detection outcome.

======================================================================
5. WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT CAN AND CANNOT ESTABLISH
======================================================================

CAN establish: whether the PTS architecture is able to exploit a
per-product routing observable, once one exists, to separate clean from
counterfeit products that share a custody hop.

CANNOT establish: that real counterfeit products exhibit this signature,
or the magnitude at which they exhibit it. The detection rate this
produces is conditional on an injected effect size chosen by the authors.
It is evidence about the scoring architecture's capacity, NOT a
measurement of real-world detection performance, and must never be
reported as the latter.

MANDATORY DISCLOSURE, to travel with any number this produces:
"Counterfeit-labelled products were additionally given a shortened custody
chain (one skipped upstream handoff) in the data generator, motivated by
the documented concentration of counterfeit entry at the wholesale
distribution tier; the resulting detection rates are therefore conditional
on an injected routing signature and are not independent evidence that the
architecture would detect real counterfeits at this rate."

======================================================================
6. AMENDMENTS AFTER FIRST EXECUTION
======================================================================

NO AMENDMENTS. The perturbation (one skipped upstream handoff) and the
detector feature (beta = 0.30) were executed exactly as frozen in
sections 2-3. Neither was adjusted after any number was observed.

======================================================================
7. RESULT — executed once, 2026-08-25
======================================================================

Design seeds 42-51 (129 min), held-out seeds 57-61 (66 min).
Held-out block is 57-61 because 52-56 were consumed by OPTION7.

                seeds   n  variant        TPR@0.75  FPR@0.75     AUC  FPR@matched
  design        42-51  10  s1_only          91.68%    11.85%  0.9132     11.72%
  design        42-51  10  s1_x_deficit     95.36%    18.97%  0.9234     16.35%
  held-out      57-61   5  s1_only          91.57%    12.19%  0.9104     12.03%
  held-out      57-61   5  s1_x_deficit     95.23%    19.40%  0.9219     15.56%

  matched-TPR FPR delta: design  +4.63 pp (std 0.30), worse on 10/10 seeds
                         held-out +3.52 pp (std 0.91), worse on  5/5 seeds

VERDICT: NULL — and worse than null. The hop-deficit feature raises AUC
(+0.010 design, +0.012 held-out) while making false-positive rate at the
registered operating point substantially WORSE, unanimously across all
15 seeds. This is the third consecutive fix to improve a threshold-free
ranking statistic while degrading or failing to improve the operating
point, after OPTION5 (clean null) and OPTION7 (AUC +0.057, matched FPR
+1.53/+1.97 pp).

MECHANISM: the hop-deficit multiplier penalizes every short custody
chain. Clean products have short chains too -- chain length varies widely
for legitimate products (median 7, p95 10) -- so the feature suppresses
S1 for a large population of legitimate items alongside the counterfeits
it targets. It sharpens the global ranking while adding noise precisely
in the high-sensitivity region where the system is specified to operate.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES: the limitation is NOT that the simulation lacks a
per-product signal. One was injected, at a magnitude fixed in advance
from a documented real-world mechanism, and the scoring layer still could
not convert it into operating-point specificity. The constraint therefore
sits in the multiplicative, organization-level PTS aggregation rather
than in the data.

The mandatory disclosure in section 5 applies to every number above.
