1. THE REDESIGN ACHIEVED COUPLING ON THE AI-ONLY CHANNEL, AND THE

   Scope: established under clustered injection. Under uniform injection
   the integrated channel still cannot respond (0.11 pp reachable), which

   custody_trust was itself a severity-linked proxy with slope 0.90, so
   provenance was the strongest signal available and weighting it 2.5:1

   realized custody history it sits at ~0.974 and is near-uniform under
   uniform injection, pinning PTS at ~0.84 — above the 0.75 alert
   threshold — so BCT-AI cannot fire regardless of what the CNN reports.
   Hence 0.00%, which is a structural zero rather than poor performance —
   structural AT THE ADOPTED PROVENANCE PENALTY OF 0.10. [Qualifier added

   level and therefore how far the composite sits from the alert
   threshold. It was fixed against gate R8 with no detection rate
   consulted, but it was NOT swept, and the feasibility spike showed S1
   moves sharply with it (mean S1 0.976 at penalty 0.10 versus 0.887 at
   0.50 under uniform injection). "Structural" is precise only with the
   constant named, and the successor pre-registration freezes the same
   constant, so the qualifier propagates there too. See L-5.]

3. BLOCKCHAIN PROVENANCE CONTRIBUTES ONLY UNDER SOURCE-CONCENTRATED
   COUNTERFEITING. Under uniform injection no organization accumulates a
   distinguishing prior record, every trust score converges (spread
   0.0156), and provenance is correctly uninformative. Under clustered
   injection the compromised manufacturers are identifiable from prior
   history alone (spread 0.3000) and BCT-AI reaches 92.29% against

4. THE ISOLATION FOREST DOES NOT CONTRIBUTE TO COUNTERFEIT DETECTION
   (0.42 pp), and should not be expected to. Counterfeit rows are not
   temperature-excursion rows, so the forest scores nominal cold-chain
   data for every counterfeit it sees. This is a domain boundary, not a
   defect, and it matches the original diagnosis that the Isolation Forest
   serves cold chain rather than counterfeit detection.
