OPTION9 — NON-MULTIPLICATIVE PTS AGGREGATION: DESIGN PRE-COMMITMENT
Written: 2026-08-26, BEFORE implementation and BEFORE any TPR/FPR observed.
Status: DESIGN FROZEN. Prediction recorded in section 3 in advance.

======================================================================
1. FROZEN FORMULA
======================================================================

composition = "mean": S1 = unweighted arithmetic mean of the per-hop trust
scores along a product's realized custody chain,

    S1 = (1/n) * sum_i trust_i

Plain arithmetic mean, computed directly (not in log space), unweighted.

NO HOP WEIGHTING. No recency weighting, no organization-type weighting.
Adding a weighting scheme after seeing whether the unweighted version
helps is exactly the move this discipline forbids, so none is specified
and none may be added within this experiment.

Justification, independent of any FPR outcome: a product is AND-composition
-- the chain scores only as well as its worst hop, and each additional hop
compounds. Trust-propagation systems (EigenTrust-style eigenvector
aggregation, Dempster-Shafer evidence combination) generally use weighted
or evidence-combining composition rather than strict multiplicative AND,
precisely so a single poor component cannot overwhelm otherwise-strong
evidence. Substituting a mean is therefore a different security semantics,
not a reparametrization of the same one.

3. PREDICTION RECORDED IN ADVANCE — THIS ONE IS LIKELY A NULL, AND WHY
======================================================================

Stated before running, because a pre-registration that omits an expected
outcome is not doing its job.

Organization trust in this system is effectively BINARY. Under clustered
injection exactly one organization is ever penalized (measured: 1 of 12);
the other eleven have a burn-in counterfeit rate of exactly 0.000000, so
their excess is negative and the lower clip at 0.0 pins them at trust
exactly 1.0. Let h = count of low-trust hops, n = chain length. Then:

    product          = 0.7^h            -> monotone in h
    geometric mean   = 0.7^(h/n)        -> monotone in h/n
    ARITHMETIC MEAN  = 1 - 0.3*(h/n)    -> ALSO monotone in h/n

The arithmetic mean therefore ranks by the SAME statistic the geometric
mean ranks by. Measured directly before freezing this document, seeds
42 and 43:

    spearman(arithmetic, geometric) = 0.999993
    spearman(arithmetic, -h/n)      = 0.999993
    spearman(geometric,  -h/n)      = 1.000000
    distinct values: product 6, geometric 41, arithmetic 41
    range: arithmetic [0.700, 1.000], geometric [0.700, 1.000]

PREDICTION: C1 will closely reproduce OPTION7's geometric-mean result --
AUC above the product control, matched-TPR FPR at or slightly worse than
the product control. It will NOT fix the operating-point FPR.

The two scores are not perfectly identical in the composite, because
0.7^u and 1-0.3u differ in curvature and the composite mixes S1 with S8
non-monotonically. That residual is the only room for C1 to differ, and it
is small. The experiment is run anyway: three prior predictions in this
line of work were partly wrong, and a measured null is worth more than an
analytical one.

CONSEQUENCE IF THE PREDICTION HOLDS: the blocker is not the choice of
aggregation function. It is that the TRUST FUNCTION IS BINARY, which
collapses every per-hop aggregation into a function of h and n alone.
That binariness is produced by the lower clip (clip(..., lower=0.0)) which
pins 11 of 12 organizations at exactly 1.0 -- the same clip identified in
the OPTION5 diagnosis. Aggregation cannot recover information the trust
function never encoded.
