OPTION10 — CONTINUOUS ORGANIZATION TRUST: DESIGN PRE-COMMITMENT
Written: 2026-08-27, BEFORE implementation and BEFORE any TPR/FPR observed.
Status: DESIGN FROZEN. Predictions in section 4 recorded in advance.

======================================================================
1. FROZEN FORMULA
======================================================================

    shrunk_rate = (n_org * org_rate + k * base_rate) / (n_org + k),  k = median(n_org)
    excess      = clip((shrunk_rate - base_rate) / base_rate, -cap, +cap),  cap = 3.0
    trust       = 1 - penalty * excess,  penalty = 0.10   ->  trust in [0.70, 1.30]

Differences from the registered behaviour, both required:
  (a) shrinkage is ON (not optional). Un-clipping RAW rates alone would be a
      foregone null: all eleven non-hot organizations have a burn-in
      counterfeit rate of exactly 0.000000, so (0 - base)/base is the SAME
      negative number for all eleven -- un-clipping would move them together
      to an identical negative value and differentiate nothing. Shrinkage is
      what makes them differ, because it weights by n_org. This reasoning is
      recorded here INSTEAD of spending held-out seed budget confirming it.
  (b) the lower clip at 0.0 is replaced by a SYMMETRIC bound at -cap, chosen
      for boundedness and interpretability: trust stays in a closed interval
      symmetric about 1.0, so an organization far below base rate earns a
      bounded bonus rather than an unbounded one.

S1 AGGREGATION: unchanged -- raw product of per-hop trust, clipped to [0,1]
ONLY AFTER aggregation, so per-hop grading is not destroyed before it can
accumulate.

======================================================================
3. MEASURED BEFORE FREEZING — WHAT CONTINUITY ACTUALLY BUYS
======================================================================

Measured on seeds 42-43 before this document was frozen:

  the eleven zero-rate organizations, under shrinkage + un-clipping:
      trust range  [1.049592, 1.050387]   SPREAD = 0.000795  (seed 42)
      trust range  [1.049163, 1.050339]   SPREAD = 0.001176  (seed 43)
      hot organization trust = 0.7000; gap to the clean cluster = 0.3500
      -> grading among the eleven is 0.23%-0.34% OF THAT GAP

So the "continuous grading among organizations" this option is named for is
numerically negligible. n_org spread is only 3-5% because the generator
distributes transactions near-uniformly across the twelve organizations,
and shrinkage can differentiate only to the extent volumes differ.

HOWEVER -- and this was not anticipated by the prompt -- un-clipping has a
SECOND, larger effect. Trust for non-hot organizations becomes 1.05, not
1.0, so it compounds multiplicatively along the chain:

      S1 = 0.7^h * 1.05^(n-h)  =>  log S1 = n*log(1.05) + h*log(0.667)

Chain length n now enters the score with a POSITIVE coefficient. Measured
on seed 42 under the frozen formula:

      distinct S1 values = 3,778   (product: 6, geometric/arithmetic mean: 41)
      spearman(S1, -h)   = 0.8590   (was 1.0000 for the raw product)
      spearman(S1, -h/n) = 0.9316   (was 1.0000 for both means)
      S1 mean: counterfeit 0.7861, clean 0.9369
      saturated at 1.0: 12.1% of counterfeit rows, 65.9% of clean rows

This is therefore the FIRST variant tested in this line of work whose
ranking is not a monotone function of h or h/n. OPTION9 failed partly
because the arithmetic mean was rank-equivalent to OPTION7's geometric mean
(Spearman 0.999993). That objection does not apply here.

4. PREDICTIONS, RECORDED IN ADVANCE
======================================================================

P1. TPR at the frozen 0.75 alert threshold will FALL substantially, because
    S1 is inflated across the board (counterfeit mean 0.604 -> 0.786). This
    is a scale shift, not a capability change, and is the same artefact seen
    in OPTION7 and OPTION9. It says nothing on its own.

P2. Matched-TPR FPR is GENUINELY UNCERTAIN, and this is stated as a real
    uncertainty rather than a hedge. Two mechanisms pull opposite ways:
      - FOR improvement: chain length now enters with a positive
        coefficient, and counterfeits have SHORTER chains (measured 6.93
        against 7.67 hops among hot-touching products, chain-length
        AUC 0.42). Every prior variant either ignored chain length or
        encoded it backwards. This is the first time it enters correctly.
      - AGAINST improvement: trust remains ORGANIZATION-level. The hot
        organization's 88% legitimate traffic still shares a hop with its
        12% counterfeit traffic, and no grading among the other eleven
        changes that. The AUC 0.64 ceiling already established for the
        ambiguous (touches-hot-org) population still applies.

P3. If matched-TPR FPR improves by more than ~2 pp on the design seeds,
    that EXCEEDS the stated expectation and must be treated as suspicious
    until the held-out block confirms it. Do not report such a number
    before 67-71 lands.

VERDICT: FIFTH NULL, and the worst of the five. Also the FIRST attempt in
which AUC itself degraded (0.9152 -> 0.9080 design, 0.9127 -> 0.9077
held-out; C2 0.9234 -> 0.8941). Every prior mechanism at least improved
the ranking statistic while failing at the operating point. Continuous
trust is not a better ranker that the threshold cannot exploit -- it is a
worse ranker.

WHY, and this sharpens the common cause rather than adding a new one:
un-clipping gives non-hot organizations trust 1.05 rather than 1.0, which
compounds along the chain:

    log S1 = n*log(1.05) + h*log(0.667)

so chain length n enters with a POSITIVE coefficient for the first time.
That direction is correct -- counterfeits do have shorter chains (6.93
against 7.67 hops among hot-touching products). But chain length is a much
WEAKER signal (AUC 0.42, i.e. 0.58 in the useful direction) than hot-hop
count, and multiplying the two together lets the weak, noisy term dilute
the strong one. Measured before the run: spearman(S1, -h) fell from 1.0000
to 0.8590.

Adding correct information made performance worse, because a multiplicative
composite cannot weight its inputs by evidential strength. That is the
same limit the other four attempts hit, stated more precisely.
